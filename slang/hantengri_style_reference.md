## 3. 5 Công thức Hantengri (locked trong gtm_execution_v1.md)

Đây là 5 patterns tao đã extract từ analysis screenshots cũ. Adapt cho Lockedin context.

### Công thức 1: "Hot take + screenshot data"

**Format:** 1 câu opinion sắc + screenshot data on-chain (hoặc chart)

**Length:** 1 line opinion + 1-3 lines breakdown + [screenshot]

**Mechanism:** Opinion alone = "ai cũng nói được". Add screenshot = "có receipts". → Combo = authority + entertaining.

**Example Lockedin context:**
```
sui still has $17b fdv somehow

60% supply locked, 65M unlocks monthly, "ecosystem incentives" airdropping to itself

infinite sell pressure

[screenshot: sui circulating supply data]
```

**When to use:**
- On-chain anomaly mày spot được (whale move, sniper concentration, suspicious tx pattern)
- Project economics phi lý (FDV vs revenue, supply unlock schedule, dev wallet activity)
- KOL contradiction mày có proof (họ shill X tuần trước, bán X tuần này)

**Pillar match:** Shitposting (30%) + có thể bridge sang Deep/Forensic (5%) nếu data đủ depth

**Cadence:** 2-3 lần/tuần

---

### Công thức 2: "Self-deprecating retrospective"

**⚠️ Adapted heavily cho Ethan** - hantengri có thể drown vào self-pity, Ethan KHÔNG được.

**Format:** Kể chuyện thật từ Ethan + lesson sharp + framing builder-mature (không victim)

**Length:** 2-5 paragraphs

**Cadence:** **Max 1-2/tháng** (không hơn). Hantengri 1-2/tuần OK; Ethan = founder, drown self-deprecating = lose trust.

**Pre-approved stories (locked v3):**

**Story A — Builder learning từ failed gamefi 2021 (~$100K, 6 months)**
```
2021. tried to build a gamefi during the boom.

at the time i thought "if these other teams can do it, why can't i."
turns out: most of them couldn't either. they were just better at marketing the exit.

i picked a dev team i shouldn't have. lost $100k. lost 6 months. shipped nothing usable.

the lesson wasn't "gamefi was bad."
it was: in this space, due diligence on people > due diligence on tech.
every time.

i didn't become a dev úp bô guy. i became a careful one. that's the difference.
```

**Story B — Founder origin: betting on wrong chain 2023-2025**
```
2023-2025 i was on the wrong chain.

watched sol terminal wave from the sideline. axiom hit $100m in 4 months. photon. bullx. gmgn. the whole class shipped while i was building somewhere else.

i wasn't wrong to bet on sui at the time. i was wrong to keep betting after the data turned.

regret = data.
that regret became lockedin.

multi-chain native, mobile-first. built for the trades happening right now — not the chain you wish was hot.
```

**Stories DROP (KHÔNG dùng):**
- $SKILL jeet 3x story = humblebrag vibe
- Zodiac NFT rủ bạn lỗ = trust damage
- Miss whitelist Crypto Cars rug ($100M) = regret missing scam vibe
- $DOGESOCCER blame dev = victim mode

**Filter rule cho future stories:** Phải pass cả 3:
1. **Builder framing** (không victim)
2. **Owning the mistake** (không blame external)
3. **Lesson sharp** (không vague "i learned a lot")

### Công thức 4: "Self-quote retweet + new commentary"

**Format:** Quote lại post cũ của chính Ethan + add 1 layer commentary mới

**Length:** 2-3 lines + quote

**Mechanism:** Tận dụng post cũ làm context, add commentary mới làm punchline. Đặc biệt mạnh khi:
- Post cũ làm prediction → bây giờ verified đúng/sai → quote + commentary
- Post cũ ship update → bây giờ user feedback → quote + commentary
- Post cũ stat → bây giờ stat update → quote + commentary

**Example Lockedin context (timeline week 8):**
```
13% of DMs are asking when ios launches

87% never downloaded android app

weird priorities ngl

[quote: post tuần 2 "android live now, ios via PWA when apple stops being apple"]
```

**Why it works:** 
- Reuse old content = leverage existing engagement
- "Receipts" effect - mày showed mày said this trước
- Commentary mới = không repeat, có added value

**Pillar match:** Cross-pillar - có thể là Shitposting (30%) hoặc Founder Diary (20%) tuỳ commentary tone

**Cadence:** 1-2 lần/tuần (khi có post cũ ngon worth quote)

### Công thức 5: "Article hack bombastic"

**Format:** Title-style line cực kêu + body 2-5 chữ phá hi vọng

**Length:** Title 5-8 chữ + body 2-5 chữ (cực ngắn, deliberate)

**Mechanism:** Setup mimicking content marketing/clickbait → punchline crush expectation. Reader engaged vì format trông như productivity tip → punchline = anti-tip = funny.

**Example raw style hantengri:**
```
how to fix your portfolio in 1 day

you can't.
```

**Example Ethan-adapted:**
```
3 simple steps to find the next 100x token

step 1: be early
step 2: be wrong about 9 of them
step 3: hold the 10th
```

```
the only memecoin trading strategy that works in 2026

luck
```

```
how to spot the bottom

you can't.
in 6 months you'll think you should've.
```

**Pillar match:** Shitposting (30%)

**Cadence:** 1-2 lần/tuần (overused format → mất sharpness, cẩn thận cadence)

## 4. Style Rules (extracted từ hantengri, locked cho Lockedin)

### 4.1 Format consistency

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Case** | Lowercase mặc định (trừ ticker $TOKEN, tên project capitalize khi cần ý nghĩa) |
| **Punctuation** | Period cuối câu OK bỏ, comma có thể bỏ context casual |
| **Slang** | 1-2 slang/post max, không spam |
| **Hashtags** | KHÔNG dùng (trừ Brand acc Lockedin campaign-specific) |
| **Emoji** | Tối đa 1 emoji ironic max, prefer 0 emoji |
| **Length** | Variation: 2 chữ → 11 bullets, không pattern lặp |
| **CTA** | "Follow me", "check site" = never. Site link only ở Founder Diary cuối post hoặc Tutorial cuối thread |

### 4.2 Tone

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Sarcasm** | Primary tool 70%+ posts |
| **Cynicism** | KHÔNG - sarcasm sharp khác cynicism (cynicism = boring) |
| **Aggression** | KHÔNG attack thẳng KOL/project có receipts. Observation tone > attack tone |
| **Vulnerability** | OK 1-2/tháng (Story A/B), builder framing |
| **Confidence** | "Earned" - dựa trên specific experience/data, không "i'm the smartest" |
| **Apology** | Không over-apologize cho takes của mình |

### 4.3 Hantengri "rhythm" - patterns recur

1. **3-line punchline pattern**: Setup → context → punchline (e.g. "btc dump 5% / longs got flushed / the cope is louder than the chart")
2. **Single-line zinger**: 1 câu duy nhất, kết, không elaborate (e.g. "every cycle creates a new generation of bagholders")
3. **List of 3 absurdities**: 3 observations stacked, không conclusion (e.g. "$10b fdv / 13 daily users / 'we're building'")
4. **Question-no-answer**: Pose câu hỏi rhetorical, không trả lời (e.g. "wen pump.fun migrates to evm?")
5. **Stat irony**: Stat #1 + Stat #2 ngược chiều + observation (e.g. "73% traders profitable / 80% quit before / selection bias is undefeated")

## 5. Adaptation cho Ethan - Hantengri-Inspired nhưng Founder-Safe

### 5.1 Voice mode split

```
60-70% Founder Mode (sharp + builder, earned confidence)
30-40% Personality Mode (sarcasm + slang, hantengri-adjacent)
```

→ Hantengri style chủ yếu apply Personality Mode. Founder Mode dùng style "axiom doesn't follow you off the desk" (positioning sharp, not joke).

### 5.2 Pillar match

| Pillar (% mix) | Hantengri-inspired? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Shitposting (30%) | ✅ HEAVY (core inspiration) | Cook bằng 5 công thức |
| Reply game (35%) | ✅ Match shitposting tone | Reply ngắn, sarcasm, observation |
| Founder Diary (20%) | ⚠️ PARTIAL | Vulnerability OK (Story A/B), nhưng KHÔNG drown |
| Tutorial + Ship Log (10%) | ❌ NO | Casual but informative, không sarcasm-heavy |
| Deep/Forensic (5%) | ❌ NO | Data-heavy, dry humor (khác sarcasm hantengri) |

### 5.3 Lines mày KHÔNG được vượt khi cook hantengri-style

❌ **Không bash MevX** (relationship politics)
❌ **Không drown self-pity** (founder phải có competence signal)
❌ **Không attack KOL by name** (cabal politics + future relationship)
❌ **Không "everyone is dumb" tone** (alienate audience)
❌ **Không doom-posting nonstop** (audience tired)
❌ **Không tham gia drama không liên quan** (vd politics, war - xem session rule "Zone Red")

✅ **Được**:
- Sarcasm về market state, project (Axiom, Photon, BullX không phải MevX), KOL behavior generic
- Observation về CT meta (reply guys, shilling patterns)
- Self-deprecating Story A/B 1-2/tháng
- Stat irony, screenshot-based hot takes
- Self-quote retweet với commentary mới

- [ ] **Avoid attack thẳng KOL by name?**
